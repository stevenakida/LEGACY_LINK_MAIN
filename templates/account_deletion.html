Delete your LegacyLink Africa account

LegacyLink Africa app · account and data deletion

You can delete your LegacyLink Africa account, and the data tied to it, at any time. Deleting is permanent and happens straight away. It can’t be undone and we can’t recover the account afterwards.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the LegacyLink Africa app (or this website) and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm. If you signed up with Google, type DELETE instead.
  4. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

You can also go straight to the deletion page: after you sign in, {{ request.scheme }}://{{ request.get_host }}{% url 'delete_account' %} opens the confirmation form.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photo, schools and contact details
  • Your posts, reposts, comments and likes
  • Your connections and connection requests
  • Your chats and the messages in them
  • Photos you uploaded, and your notifications

What may remain

  • Residual copies may remain in our secure backups for a limited period before they are overwritten.
  • Content that other people have already received from you elsewhere, such as a screenshot someone took, is outside our control.

Can’t sign in?

If you can’t sign in, email us from the address on your account at developer@legacylink.africa with the phone number or email you registered with and ask us to delete your account. We will check that the account is yours before deleting it.

Administrator accounts can’t be deleted from the Settings page; ask another administrator to remove them.
